# Steam Workshop Description Guidelines & Reference

This document provides formatting rules, length limits, and reference templates for maintaining the Steam Workshop listing for **Immersive Namelists Expanded - Addon: Larger Selector**.

---

## Core Rules & Invariants

1. **No Emojis**: Strictly avoid emojis anywhere in the description text.
2. **Character Limits**: Steam Workshop descriptions have a max limit of ~17,000 raw BBCode characters. Keep bullet points concise and direct.
3. **No Planned Section**: Do not include speculative 'Planned' sections in the public description.
4. **Valid Steam BBCode**:
   - Headers: `[h1]Title[/h1]`
   - Bold: `[b]Text[/b]`
   - Italics: `[i]Text[/i]`
   - Links: `[url=https://...]Text[/url]`
   - Bullets: Standard hyphen (`- Item`)

---

## Template Description

```bbcode
[h1]Immersive Namelists Expanded - Addon: Larger Selector[/h1]
A lightweight quality-of-life UI addon for Hearts of Iron IV that expands and widens the division namelist selection dropdown in the Division Designer.

[h1]Features:[/h1]
- Greatly expanded dropdown window showing significantly more namelists at once.
- Wider entry slots preventing historical unit labels from truncating.
- Smooth, faster scroll navigation through extensive namelist collections.
- Designed as an ideal companion for [b]Immersive Namelists Expanded[/b] and large namelist overhaul packs.
- Fully compatible with Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.*.
- 100% Ironman, Achievement, and Multiplayer compatible (Checksum neutral).

[h1]Compatibility:[/h1]
- Compatible with all namelist and historical flavour mods.
- Modifies [i]interface/divisiondesignerview.gui[/i]. May conflict with overhaul mods that completely redesign the division designer window layout.
- Safe to enable or disable mid-campaign.

[h1]Feedback & Contributions:[/h1]
Please report issues, suggestions, or feedback in the Steam Workshop discussion threads.
```
